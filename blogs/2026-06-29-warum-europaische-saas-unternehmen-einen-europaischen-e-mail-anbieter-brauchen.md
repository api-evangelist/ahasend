European SaaS companies should prioritize European email providers because transactional emails contain personal data covered by GDPR, making the email provider an important data processor. While server location matters, the critical issue is legal jurisdiction, since US companies can access data stored in Europe under the CLOUD Act regardless of physical location. The article argues European providers offer compliance advantages without sacrificing deliverability or cost.
